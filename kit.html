Book text

The harbour light

The keeper climbed the stairs each evening at the same hour. She counted the steps without thinking, one hundred and four to the lamp room, and trimmed the wick before the last colour left the sky. From the gallery she could see the whole bay, the fishing boats turning for home and the long grey line of the breakwater.

Her logbook lay open on the desk. Wind from the north-west, a moderate sea, visibility good. She had written the same words on many nights, and the notes at the end of the book say that the entries continue for thirty years. Nothing in them tells you what she thought about.

A highlight can also hold a link to a note. This sentence has the moving marker that follows the voice, and this part looks like a text selection.
